How working with me goes

  1. You contact me. Tell me about your business and what you need.
  2. I send a written quotation. It states the scope, revisions and delivery time.
  3. You pay the deposit. I start once it is confirmed in my M-Pesa records.
  4. I share a private preview. You review it and send feedback.
  5. You approve and pay the balance. The remaining amount is paid before handover.
  6. Handover. Once the balance is confirmed, I hand over your site and show you how to use it.

Common questions

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size of the site. Every quotation includes an estimated delivery time, counted from when your deposit and content are received.

What do I need to give you?

Your business name, contact details, logo if you have one, photos and the text you want shown. If you are missing some of these, I can advise you.

Do I pay before you start?

Yes. A deposit is paid before work begins, and the balance is paid before the final website is handed over.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Each quotation includes a set number of revision rounds. Extra changes or new features are quoted separately.

Who owns the website?

Once the full price is paid, the finished website is yours. Domain and hosting fees are paid yearly.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Every site is designed to work on phones, tablets and computers.
